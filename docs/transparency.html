Why publish this?

Because a raw holder count and an independent-holder count can describe two very different realities. LANDING uses the stricter interpretation. The project does not count its own allocation wallets, founder wallets, other known operational wallets or the liquidity pool as organic community adoption.

Classification rule.

Project allocations remain identified as project-controlled even after operational transfers. A wallet is not treated as an independent holder merely because it appears as a separate positive token account. This prevents LANDING from inflating its own adoption statistics.
