RouteLANDING / USDT

Buy LANDING without touching a wallet.

Poloniex lists LANDING against USDT. If you have never used a Solana wallet, this is the shorter path in, with one trade-off you should understand before you take it.

Open PoloniexCompare routes
01 / Walkthrough

From nothing to holding.

01

Create and verify an account

Exchanges require identity verification. How long it takes varies by country and by how busy they are, so start it before you want to trade rather than after.

02

Deposit USDT

Send USDT from another exchange or buy it on Poloniex directly. Make sure the network you withdraw on matches the network the deposit address expects. Mismatched networks are the most common way people lose a deposit.

03

Find the LANDING / USDT market

Search for LANDING in the markets list and open the LANDING/USDT pair.

04

Use a limit order

A market order fills at whatever the book offers, which on a thin market can be a long way from the price you just looked at. A limit order sets the worst price you will accept and waits.

Slower, occasionally unfilled, usually cheaper.

05

Decide whether to withdraw

Tokens on an exchange are held by the exchange. That is convenient, and it means the balance is their record of what you own rather than yours.

To hold LANDING yourself, withdraw to a Solana wallet address. Send a small test amount first and confirm it arrives before moving the rest.

02 / Context

What a listing does not mean.

Exchange listings get read as endorsements. They are not endorsements, and pretending otherwise would be the exact behaviour this project exists to avoid.

It is a venue, not a verdict

A listing means a market exists. It is not an exchange telling you a token is sound, and it carries no opinion about price.

It does not make the token less speculative

Every risk on the rest of this site applies identically to tokens bought here.

The price will differ from the pool

The exchange book and the on-chain pool are separate markets and routinely disagree. Neither is the true price.

Prefer to hold your own keys?

The Jupiter route swaps from your own wallet and never asks you to verify your identity with anyone.
