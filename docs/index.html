Our story.

Every serious crypto project needs an origin story. Ours is refreshingly less impressive.

🪂

We had nothing to do.
So we made a coin.

No problem to solve. No industry to disrupt. No whitepaper explaining why humanity needed another token.

Landing Coin started with an idea, some curiosity, too much free time and the observation that apparently every crypto project wants to go to the moon.

So we decided to go in the opposite direction.

Down.

Preferably with a parachute.

🎯

A mission without the moon speech.

Landing Coin intends to support future experiments and projects connected to the blockchain world.

Part of the project's resources may be used over time to explore ideas, prototypes, tools and initiatives we believe are worth building.

That does not mean guaranteed funding, guaranteed projects or guaranteed returns.

We prefer experiments over promises.
